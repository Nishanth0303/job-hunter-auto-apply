How 1-Click Auto-Apply Works
Why Portals Need Authentication: Portals like Wellfound, Naukri, and LinkedIn require you to be signed in to submit job applications to your personal candidate profile. A standalone website cannot secretly submit forms into your account without a browser extension or bookmarklet.
Option 1: Chrome Extension (Fully Automated)
  1. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
  2. Enable Developer Mode (top right toggle).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extension folder in this project repository.
  4. Now whenever you click "Approve & Apply", the job opens and the extension automatically pastes your customized cover letter and triggers the Apply button!
Option 2: 1-Click Auto-Apply Bookmarklet (Instant, No Installation)
  1. Drag the blue "⭐ Drag: ⚡ 1-Click Auto-Apply Bookmarklet" button directly onto your Chrome Bookmarks Bar.
  2. When you open any job page (e.g. Wellfound, Naukri, LinkedIn), click that bookmark in your bookmarks bar.
  3. It instantly injects your customized cover note into the application box and clicks Apply!
